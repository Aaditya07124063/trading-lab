Zerodha charges you only 0.01% of the turnover as brokerage or a maximum of Rs. 20 per executed order.
There are other charges which are explained below.

Charge Structure Explained:

STT/CTT: This is charged only on the sell side for intraday and F&O trades. It is charged on both sides for Delivery trades in Equity.
Transaction/Turnover Charges: Exchange turnover charges + other charges (including clearing charges)
Service Tax: This is charged at 12 % of the total cost of brokerage plus turnover charges.
Education Cess: This is charged at 2% of the Service Tax.
Higher Education Cess: This is charged at 1% of the Service Tax.
SEBI Charges: This is charged at Rs 10 per crore.

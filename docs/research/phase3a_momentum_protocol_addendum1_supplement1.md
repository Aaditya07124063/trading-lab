## The seven rulings

1. The account is a retail individual account.
2. Where an archived document states the date a rate took effect, that date is used. Where only dated page captures exist and two consecutive captures differ, the higher-cost value applies to every date between them. A capture is not evidence of an effective date.
3. The NSE scheme of 1 August 2020 to 31 March 2021 for stocks outside NIFTY 50 and NIFTY Next 50 is not applied; the standard structure applies to every stock.
4. For 2016–2019 the depository charge of ₹13.5 is treated as before tax, and the indirect tax in force is added. This is an assumption.
5. A temporary change to the SEBI fee is applied only for dates fixed by an archived SEBI instrument.
6. Before 1 April 2023 the IPFT contribution is applied only for dates on which an archived NSE document establishes its amount; otherwise the §13 fallback governs. The amount called "existing" in March 2023 is not used for earlier dates. The January 2021 increase in the transaction charge is not added a second time.
7. For stamp duty before 1 July 2020 the §13 fallback applies as written. It charges the buyer only, whereas duty then fell on both sides at state rates; this is disclosed with every result that uses it.

## Disclosures that go with these rulings

- **Ruling 1** may favour the strategy: zero delivery brokerage from 1 December 2015 applies to retail individual accounts only; other account types continued to pay the lower of 0.1% or ₹20 per order.
- **Ruling 7** may favour the strategy: the fallback omits the sell-side duty that applied before July 2020.
- Rulings 2, 3, 4 and 5 take the higher-cost reading.
